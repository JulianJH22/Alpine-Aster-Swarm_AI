# Alpine Aster — Leukerbad Hackathon

## Project
**IA Enjambre:** cooperative robotics for simulated disassembly and material sorting.

## Demo
Two arms stabilize a central object; two perform scripted component removal; two sort components onto a rotating six-sector platform; the platform routes components to metal and plastic conveyor belts.

## AI research
Behavior cloning (BC) and MAPPO have been evaluated for local TCP positioning. They do not yet execute autonomous end-to-end disassembly.
